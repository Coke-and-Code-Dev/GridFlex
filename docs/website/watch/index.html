The problem and the product.

Why charging on arrival can overwhelm a depot's connection, and how GridFlex plans around the power it already has.

Pitch summary

From a charging peak to a plan.

Why timing matters

The film follows ten delivery vehicles plugging in together. Their combined charging creates a peak on a connection already serving the building.

What the schedule changes

GridFlex uses the time between arrival and departure to stagger charging. Vans that can wait leave room for vans that need energy sooner.

How the depot decides

The schedule is replayed against unseen building demand. The operator can see breaches and energy shortfalls before deciding what to try.

The depot and charging figures in the film are illustrative. Explore the pipeline to see how an actual GridFlex run turns inputs into evidence.
